Replay search

Games the league played that tagpro.eu has no record of. TagPro itself records every game, and anyone signed in can look through the games they played in — so if you were in one of these, its replay may still be in your list.

If you played in one of these:
  1. Open tagpro.koalabeast.com/replays and sign in, then look under My Games.
  2. Set the date range to the night below and find the game by its map and the other games around it.
  3. Send the link (or the file) to the archive and it gets added here.
