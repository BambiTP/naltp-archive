NALTP Archive

The North American TagPro leagues: Majors, Minors and Novice, every season.

  • Seasons each season's teams, schedule and results, draft, rosters and trades
  • Coverage every week of every season: which games are on tagpro.eu, which are lost, and why

Most useful

  • archive.json (379 KB) every season, week and matchup with its tagpro.eu match numbers, and nothing else: {season: {week: {"Team A v Team B": [match ids]}}}. Playoff rounds come after the weeks, by name
  • eus.zip (71 MB) every one of those tagpro.eu matches as a file, laid out the same way: season / week / Team A v Team B / <match id>.json, with archive.json inside. Each file is the match as tagpro.eu serves it, so getStatsFromEU can read a whole season or week folder

Downloads

  • games.json (26 MB) every game: seasons, weeks, matchups, scores, each tagpro.eu match and its line-ups
  • games.csv (6 MB) the same games as games.json, one row per tagpro.eu match (or per game where there's none), regular season and playoffs
  • matchups.csv (488 KB) every matchup and playoff series: games owed, games we have, and how many are lost, not played or unknown, with why
  • draft.csv (1 MB) every draft pick
  • rosters_after_draft.csv (2 MB) every team's players after the draft
  • rosters_end_of_season.csv (495 KB) every team's players at the end of the season
  • playoff_series.csv (260 KB) every playoff series
  • playoff_champions.csv (26 KB) every champion
  • missing_games.csv (26 KB) only the matchups with a game missing or an extra match, with what we have of each game and why
  • games_index.json (22 MB) every game, its tagpro.eu match and replay files in the zips below, and each week's missing or extra games
  • pastebin_records.zip (531 KB) every match saved to pastebin

Every game's files

  • NALTP_games_tagpro_eu.zip every game in a season / week / matchup / game folder: its full tagpro.eu match file, an info.txt, and a txt per week saying what's missing or extra (100 MB)
  • NALTP_games_replays_S31-S39.zip TagPro's replays, with player positions, of 3,737 S31-S39 games, in the same folders: unzip it in the same place as the one above (2 GB)
